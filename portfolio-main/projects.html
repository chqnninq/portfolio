Automated Profilometry System

UBC Electron Microscopy Laboratory · ExpMicroMech · 2026 · NSERC USRA

Over four months I designed and built an automated profilometry system to drive surface topography mapping in UBC's robot-enabled Electron Microscopy Laboratory. The system lets a user map the surface topography of a sample and measure how much material has been removed from it.

Building it meant modelling components in SolidWorks and writing a Python control architecture to integrate a confocal sensor and a precision stage into a motorized optical microscope. A Raspberry Pi orchestrates the motorized movement of the camera and interferometer assembly; a Keyence CL-P015 confocal sensor handles the surface topography mapping, with data processed through Gwyddion and linked back to the lab's robot-enabled SEM suite.

To prove the instrument was trustworthy, I ran metrology measurement DOEs and repeatability and reproducibility studies to validate micron-level accuracy, and designed and manufactured the mounting brackets for mechanical integration.

To show what it produces: a full topography scan and 3-D map of a Canadian quarter, measuring the heights of 144,400 points across its surface (map credit: G. Francolini).

Tools

Driver Interface Dashboard Enclosure

UBC Formula Electric (FSAE) · 2025–2026

I designed the dashboard enclosure for the driver interface of UBC Formula Electric's electric race car in SolidWorks, then validated it for waterproofing to keep it compliant with FSAE regulations. The work ran inside a fast-paced, cross-functional team using Subversion and Altium CoDesigner to keep the mechanical and electrical sides in sync.

Tools

BC Adolescent Health Survey Analysis

McCreary Centre Society · 2023–2025

I ran statistical processing of the 2023 BC Adolescent Health Survey in IBM SPSS, doing root-cause analysis across large datasets. Alongside that I worked with UBC researchers on improving the Middle Years Development Instrument provincial survey, looking for places its data collection could be tightened. The work ended in a set of policy recommendations I presented to the BC Ministry of Education and Childcare, advocating for improvements to provincial school food programs.

Tools

IBM SPSS · Survey methodology · Root cause analysis
